# Pennykin 1.3.0 — Living Collection

## Pet conversations

- Replaces the two fixed tap replies with more than thirty possible responses.
- Replies change with morning, afternoon, and evening; cat, dog, and rabbit personality; today’s check-in; pending gifts; weekly budget; savings milestones; and return after a break.
- Consecutive taps rotate through the relevant pool instead of repeating the same sentence.
- Tap reactions can also change the pet’s calm, curious, celebrating, sleeping, or gently concerned pose.
- All money-related dialogue remains non-judgmental and rewards honest recording rather than spending less.

## Redesigned pets

- Dog: new caramel-and-cocoa coat, teal collar, chest bib, larger muzzle, asymmetrical floppy ears, and a more grounded silhouette.
- Rabbit: new misty blue-gray coat, apricot inner ears, rose collar, lop-ear silhouette, larger hind feet, and a ready-to-hop pose.
- Both are transparent 600 × 640 PNGs and keep the cat’s illustration quality and gold paw tag.

## Complete 24-entry collection

- Adds Mystery Gift and Berry Biscuit Pouch as special catalog records, bringing the illustrated catalog to 24 entries.
- Every detail sheet now explicitly lists: name, rarity, obtained date, obtained method, ownership, equipped state, and item story.
- Special items correctly display **Not equippable**.
- New gift and treat discoveries are persisted in the local inventory history; existing qualifying data is backfilled during migration.

## Build identity

- Version updated to 1.3.0 in `package.json`, `package-lock.json`, and `app.json`.
- The app displays `Pennykin 1.3.0 · Living Collection build` in Insights → Settings & privacy.
