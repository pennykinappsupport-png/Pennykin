# Pennykin 1.9.0 — Simple Insights

- Rebuilt Insights around Day, Week, Month, and Year views.
- Added a combined spending bar chart and cumulative trend line.
- Added a category donut chart and ranked category bars.
- Added period totals, averages, entry counts, and Need/Want mix.
