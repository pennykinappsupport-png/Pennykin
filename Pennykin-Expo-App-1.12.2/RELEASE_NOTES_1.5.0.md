# Pennykin 1.5.0 — Weekly Budget Planner

This release turns the weekly budget from a single ceiling into a practical plan while keeping the pet-first Home screen calm and uncluttered.

## What changed

- Added category plans for Food, Transport, School, Entertainment, Shopping, and Other.
- Category allocations cannot exceed the weekly total; unused money is shown as Unallocated.
- Check-in categories automatically feed the corresponding Actual and Remaining values.
- Added category and total budget variance with neutral Above plan, Below plan, and On plan language.
- Added Safe to Spend Today using remaining budget, reserved future expenses, and days remaining including today.
- Added editable Reserved expenses for known costs such as transport, lunch, or weekend activities.
- Added a seven-day spending forecast with a low-data waiting state and an estimate disclaimer.
- Replaced the two old Home finance cards with one concise Safe to Spend Today card.
- Moved savings goal, achievements, beta feedback, Founders Pro, profile, and privacy controls into a secondary Settings page.
- Category plans and reserved expenses remain local in SQLite and are included in full local-data deletion.
