# Pennykin 1.5.1 — Focused Insights

This update removes repeated information from Insights and keeps financial planning features close to the data they explain.

## What changed

- Replaced the separate Spent, Needs, Wants, and Forecast cards with one Weekly Overview.
- Added a compact Needs/Wants percentage bar inside Weekly Overview.
- Kept the weekly forecast compact by default; tapping it reveals or hides its explanation and estimate disclaimer.
- Removed the separate Budget Variance card and embedded its neutral summary in Weekly Budget Planner.
- Moved Savings Goal from Settings to Insights with a tappable progress card and direct saved-amount editing.
- Moved Achievements from Settings to the bottom of Insights.
- Settings now contains only Beta Feedback, Founders Pro, Profile & Privacy, and Delete Data.
