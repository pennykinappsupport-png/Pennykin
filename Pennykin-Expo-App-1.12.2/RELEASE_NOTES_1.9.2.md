# Pennykin 1.9.2 — Faster Insight Navigation

## What changed

- Added a clear, tappable bar-chart shortcut beside the Home title.
- Replaced the bottom navigation’s barcode-like Insights symbol with a real three-bar chart icon.
- Made the selected Day, Week, Month, or Year title tappable.
- Added a quick-jump bottom sheet for directly selecting recent days, weeks, months, or years.
- Kept the previous/next arrows for one-period adjustments.
- Refreshed the fixed category palette with cleaner, lower-saturation yellow, blue, green, purple, pink, and gray tones.
