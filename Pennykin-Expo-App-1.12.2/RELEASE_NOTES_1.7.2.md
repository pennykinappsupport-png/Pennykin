# Pennykin 1.7.2 — Room HUD & Compact Insights

## Insights

- Safe to Spend Today and Forward-looking Reset now share one left/right card instead of stacking vertically.
- The Needs/Wants composition bar is now a vertical rail on the left side of Money Insights.
- Opportunity Cost and Budget Flexibility use two compact cards to the right of that rail.
- Spending Pace remains expandable, preserving its detailed formula and estimate without occupying space by default.

## Home

- Streak and Pet Coins moved into the room as high-contrast translucent HUD capsules.
- Added balanced Collection and room-shop shortcuts on the upper right of the room.
- Pet dialogue now floats inside the room instead of using a separate full-width card below it.
- Added a compact Fullness, Happiness, and Energy status strip.
- Added direct Feed, Play, and Clean controls below the room. These reuse the full eating, toy-grab/movement, and bath animations.
- Pet-care shortcuts stay disabled until today’s honest financial check-in is complete, preserving the existing reward boundary.
