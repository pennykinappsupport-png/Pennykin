# Pennykin App Store Copy — Draft

## Name

**Pennykin: First Budget Pet**

This is a working brand. Re-check App Store availability, domain/social handles, and USPTO or relevant national trademark records before submission.

## Subtitle

**Gentle money habits for students**

## Promotional text

Meet a tiny companion who brings home surprises when you check in honestly. Build a cozy room together—without a bank connection, account, or judgment.

## Short description

Pennykin is a local-first budget companion built by a teen for first-time budgeters. Log an expense or a no-spend day, discover mystery gifts, decorate a tiny home, notice needs versus wants, and make progress toward one savings goal.

## Key messages for screenshots

1. **Your first budget shouldn’t feel like homework** — onboarding and the privacy promises.
2. **A cozy life you build together** — room-first home screen and original pet.
3. **Three honest entries. One mystery gift.** — gift trail and opening moment.
4. **Make every corner feel like yours** — live room-item preview and collection.
5. **See this week without judgment** — weekly budget, needs/wants, and categories.
6. **Give your money a destination** — savings progress appearing in the room.
7. **Private by design** — on-device storage, no bank link, reset control.
8. **Built by a teen, for first-time budgeters** — beta positioning.
