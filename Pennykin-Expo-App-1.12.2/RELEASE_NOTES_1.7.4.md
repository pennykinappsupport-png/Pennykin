# Pennykin 1.7.4 — Unified Home & Pet

## Navigation and interaction fixes

- Removed the repetitive Pet tab and merged its essential status information into Home.
- Saving a new expense now returns directly to the Home room and plays the celebration with confetti.
- Moved the More/Fewer actions control to the bottom of the whole care panel, below all expanded actions.

## Pet status bottom sheet

- The visible 🍎 Fullness, 😊 Happiness, and ⚡ Energy strip remains unchanged.
- Tapping any of the three indicators opens a bottom sheet matching the Shop detail interaction.
- The sheet includes Fullness, Happiness, Energy, Cleanliness, Health, level, life stage, XP, Pet Coins, and care energy.
- Added accessible labels, outside-tap dismissal, a close button, and adaptive theme colors.
