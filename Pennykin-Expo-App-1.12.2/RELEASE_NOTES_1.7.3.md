# Pennykin 1.7.3 — Expandable Pet Care

## Home refinements

- Restored the “Your little home” title and “Built one honest money check-in at a time.” subtitle above the room.
- Removed the Collection and room-shop icons from the upper-right corner of the room.
- Kept the high-contrast translucent Streak and Pet Coin capsules in the upper-left corner.
- Preserved Feed, Play, and Clean as the always-visible primary actions.
- Added an accessible More actions control with a visible expanded/collapsed state.
- Expanded actions include Rest, Vet, and Special Treat.
- Rest, Vet, and Special Treat reuse their real animated pet states; they are not decorative shortcuts.
- All care actions retain the existing rule that today’s honest financial check-in must be completed first.
