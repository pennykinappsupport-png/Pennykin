# Pennykin 1.9.3 — Calendar & Home Polish

## What changed

- Moved the small chart cue from the Home title to the pet room HUD, beside 😊; both open Pet Status.
- Replaced the period list with a tap-to-select monthly calendar grid, including adjacent-month dates and a Today shortcut.
- Selecting a date jumps directly to the matching day, week, month, or year; previous/next arrows remain for small adjustments.
- Arranged all six pet actions in two rows of three.
